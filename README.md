SANDLINE is a round-based tactical FPS in three.js: plant the charge at the Solar Array or the Warehouse Yard, or wipe the Jackals, across up to 9 rounds with a buy menu, four weapons and two grenades. Eight bots — three Sandline allies and five Jackal defenders — patrol the depot, hold angles, react to sight and sound, and shoot back. Aim down sights to tighten your spread, flash a corner before you push it, and mind the loss-streak bonus when the rounds go against you.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
